The Goal

Target Product Concept

The Prototype Product: A fully autonomous, self-balancing robotic tennis ball collector. Equipped with an advanced visual sensor suite and automated propulsion, this design completely eliminates the need for manual rolling on the court.

Latest Progress — Live Demo

Watch It Search, Approach, and Collect

REC — SIM FEED

What this shows: a full autonomous cycle running in the custom 2D simulator. The robot rotates in SEARCH until the target enters its field of view, closes distance during APPROACH using proportional steering on the ball's pixel offset, completes a timed COLLECT maneuver, then automatically respawns a new target and repeats — validated across multiple consecutive cycles with no manual intervention.

System Architecture

A Closed Feedback Loop, Not a Pipeline

Three ROS 2 nodes, connected only by topic name and message type — none of them aware of how the others are implemented.

Sense

mock_camera

Captures a first-person visual feed and calculates 2D simulation geometry — robot pose, target position, and field-of-view intersection — every 20Hz timer tick.

pub /image_raw
Perceive

vision_node

Applies HSV colour segmentation and contour analysis to isolate the tennis ball, then computes its normalized pixel offset and apparent radius.

pub /ball_detection
Decide

nav_node

Runs a 3-state FSM — SEARCH → APPROACH → COLLECT — converting target offset into proportional steering and forward velocity commands.

pub /cmd_vel
/cmd_vel loops back into mock_camera, updating the simulated pose on the next tick — closing the loop every cycle.
